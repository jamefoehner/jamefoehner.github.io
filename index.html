01 / Fine-tuning & reinforcement learning

When a model learns
to game the reward.

I fine-tuned Qwen2.5-1.5B with LoRA, then tested GRPO rewards. From the base model, an accuracy reward produced all-zero answers on 66 of 89 filings while still reporting 87% field accuracy.

Fine-tuning: macro-F1 0.35 → 0.67.
Best RL run: 0.73; not statistically distinguishable from fine-tuning alone.

02 / LLM evaluation

Can language models
read between the boilerplate?

I labeled 100 filings and tested whether models distinguish actual investor demands from legal boilerplate. Written labeling rules mattered more than moving to the larger model.

83% exact match on 86 scored filings.
Model disagreements helped identify six reference-label errors.

03 / Information diffusion

Does the market
absorb the news at once?

Across 225 campaigns, I tested whether post-filing drift concentrates where attention is low. The price-delay effect survived several robustness checks; illiquidity explained the apparent size effect.

The median campaign absorbed 46% of its measured move on days 0–1. The rest unfolded over the following three weeks.

A machine learning pipeline on roughly 80,000 rows of NBA play-by-play data. Gradient boosting predicts in-game win probability (ROC-AUC 0.84; Brier score 0.071), with play classification and player-stat predictions in a Streamlit app.

Boston Airbnb Price Prediction

Benchmarked five regression models on more than 3,500 listings. A tuned random forest reduced average error from roughly $110 to $75 and improved test R² from 0.12 to 0.48. Location and host listing count emerged as leading price drivers.
